MAPS

Explore the corners of the world altered by First Light

BIND //

Bind, located in Morocco, has no middle lane. Instead, it features two one-way teleporters that allow for rapid repositioning. It is a critical facility for the Kingdom Corporation, responsible for mining and refining a mysterious substance called Radianite.

The battles agents fight here often revolve around controlling these Radianite facilities.

\\ HAVEN

Haven, located in Bhutan, is a monastery destroyed by First Light. Unlike other maps, it has three bomb sites (A, B, and C), requiring the defending team to spread out more, while giving the attacking team more tactical options.

Once a symbol of peace, it has now become a highly contested battleground due to the Radianite buried beneath it.

ASCENT //

Ascent is modeled after Venice, Italy. The entire city was lifted into the sky during the First Light event. This map features an open middle area and mechanical blast doors that can be opened or closed.

This marvel of a suspended city not only showcases the immense power of First Light but also serves as the perfect stage for agents to demonstrate their tactical prowess.
